==========================================================================
CONCLUSION
==========================================================================
Applying SHAP to an HPVc-coverage model would explain noise: the model
has no out-of-sample skill. Valid options are (a) report the negative
result as a finding, and/or (b) apply XAI to cervical-cancer mortality,
where the same determinants do carry signal.
